# Medicne_Purchasing_System

The Medicine Purchasing System is a smart vending machine that allows users to purchase medicine for their specific condition. This project utilizes to control the vending machine's functions and ensure the dispensing of the correct medication. The system is designed to provide an easy and convenient way for users to access their necessary medicine, while also ensuring the safety and accuracy of the dispensing process.
  
The introduction of the Medicine Purchasing System aims to improve the accessibility and efficiency of purchasing medicine for individuals with unhealthy conditions recurring conditions that affect a person, and this system will provide the appropriate. With the Medicine Purchasing System, people with mild headaches, stomachaches, flu and other conditions will no longer need to invest a lot of time and money in going to a hospital or clinic to buy their medicine specially for UiTM Campus Raub students. In addition, UiTM’s location that far from the city and the limited operating hours of the UiTM Health Unit make it difficult for UiTM students to get their medicine. So,with this system will save them a lot of time, energy, and money.

Furthermore, the Medicine Purchasing System will be equipped with an advanced security system to protect the privacy and personal information of the users. The system will be designed to encrypt and secure the user's personal and medical information, ensuring that it is protected from hacking or breaches with only 3 attempt will be given to login. This will give the users peace of mind that their information is safe and secure.

In summary, the Medicine Purchasing System is a smart vending machine that aims to improve the accessibility and efficiency of purchasing medicine for individuals. It utilizes to control the vending machine's functions, ensuring the dispensing of the correct medication and safety and accuracy of the dispensing process. It will also be user-friendly, easy to use, and equipped with advanced security features to protect users' personal and medical information.

Project Objectives:-
• To apply the lesson syllabus in the use of selection structure, repetition, functions andarray in building programs.
• Take over the duties of the health unit when it is closed, especially after officehours and on weekends.
• Save time and cost without having to go to the pharmacy or store to buy medicine especially for students.
